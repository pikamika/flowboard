Flowboard – team Kanban board

Flowboard helps teams plan and track work on a shared board with four columns: Backlog, In Progress, In Review and Done.

Features

  • Cards with a title, assignee, priority (High, Medium, Low) and due date
  • Drag cards between columns or use the keyboard-friendly Move menu
  • Delete with undo, and live sync between teammates

Sign in to open your board. JavaScript is required to use the app.
